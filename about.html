About Me

Finance student and aspiring investor. Occasionally lucky, always curious.

My academic journey started at the Vrije Universiteit Brussel, where I pursued a Bachelor in Business Economics with the hopes and dreams of becoming an entrepreneur. After graduating, I continued with a Master in Business Engineering at KU Leuven, majoring in Risk and Finance.

Somewhere along the way, I came to appreciate the hard truth that having a great idea or product is only one part of building a successful business. My interest gradually shifted towards the financial side of things: how businesses create value, how they are financed, and ultimately, what they are worth. Taking more practically oriented finance courses during my exchange in Canada only reinforced that interest and convinced me that I wanted to pursue a career in finance, with a particular interest in venture capital, private equity, and investing more broadly. My time abroad also made me realize how important relevant experience is in this industry.

Which brings me to today.
I'm currently pursuing an additional Master's in Financial Economics at KU Leuven, with the goal of making the most of the time I have before entering the job market. That means trying to land internships, joining relevant student organizations, building this website, and doing as much independent work as I can or, more generally, trying anything that I think will make me better at what I want to do.

The goal of this website isn't to provide financial advice or to show off what I know. It's simply a place to put what I've learned into practice. I want to use it to document where I am today, keep track of what I'm learning, and, hopefully, look back in a few years and see how much I've improved.
